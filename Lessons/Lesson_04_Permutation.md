# Lesson 04: Permutation and Combination

## Introduction
In this lesson, we will learn about counting principles used in Statistics.

### 1. What is Permutation?
Permutation is the arrangement of objects in a specific order.

Formula: 
P(n,r) = n! / (n-r)!

Example: 
How many ways can 3 students be arranged from 5 students?
P(5,3) = 5! / 2! = 60 ways.

### 2. What is Combination?
Combination is the selection of objects without considering order.

Formula:
C(n,r) = n! / r! * (n-r)!

Example:
How many ways to choose 2 fruits from 4 fruits?
C(4,2) = 6 ways.

### 3. Key Difference
- Permutation = Order Matters
- Combination = Order Does NOT Matter
